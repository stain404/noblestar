Noble Star Shipping offers smooth connectivity for LCL cargo across major shipping routes
throughout the GCC. Less than container load consolidation lets you ship modest volumes
at sea freight economics — you buy space in a shared container instead of paying for a
box you cannot fill.

## Who LCL suits

LCL is the right choice when you are moving somewhere between one and roughly thirteen
cubic metres. Below one cubic metre, courier or air freight is often simpler and not much
more expensive. Above thirteen, a 20ft container usually costs less and moves faster.

## What the service includes

- **Consolidation** at origin, with your cargo received, checked and manifested
- **Ocean leg** on scheduled sailings with our co-loading partners
- **Deconsolidation** at destination
- **Customs clearance** filed in-house
- **Final delivery** to the consignee's door

## Understanding revenue tonne

LCL is priced on whichever is greater: the cargo's volume in cubic metres, or its weight
in tonnes. Three cubic metres weighing one tonne is charged as three revenue tonnes. One
cubic metre weighing three tonnes is also charged as three. This catches out first-time
shippers of dense goods — send us both figures and we will give you the real number
rather than an optimistic one.

## Packing advice

Palletise where you can, shrink-wrap, and mark every piece clearly with the consignee and
your reference. LCL cargo is handled at consolidation, at the port, at deconsolidation
and at delivery. Packaging that survives an FCL move may not survive an LCL one.
