Sea freight is where Noble Star does most of its work — full containers and consolidated
cargo through every major container gateway, with contracted space on top-tier carriers
and full visibility from booking to delivery.

## What the service includes

Every booking is one file: one reference, one coordinator, one invoice covering as much
of the chain as you need.

- **Origin** — collection from your supplier, export clearance, container stuffing and
  terminal handling
- **Ocean leg** — carrier booking, bill of lading, and cargo insurance if you want it
  arranged
- **Destination** — import clearance, port charges and delivery to the consignee's door

## Cargo we handle

General and palletised cargo, project and out-of-gauge shipments, reefer cargo, liquid
bulk in flexitanks or ISO tanks, and food consignments requiring inspection. Hazardous
cargo is accepted subject to class, UN number and carrier approval — tell us at the
quoting stage.

## FCL or LCL

Past roughly 13–15 cubic metres, a 20ft container usually beats LCL rates per cubic
metre and removes the consolidation steps, which shortens transit. Below that, LCL is
almost always the better answer. We quote both when it is close.

## Getting a quote

Send the origin and destination, the commodity, the total weight and volume or container
count, your incoterm, and the date the cargo is ready. If any of that is uncertain, send
what you have.
