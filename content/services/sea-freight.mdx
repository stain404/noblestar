Sea freight remains the backbone of GCC trade, and it is where Noble Star does most of
its work. We move full containers and consolidated cargo through the region's major
gateways — Jebel Ali, Khalifa Port, Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam,
Hamad Port and Sohar — with contracted space on top-tier carriers and full visibility
from booking to delivery.

## What the service includes

Every sea freight booking is managed as a single file. That means one reference number,
one coordinator, and one invoice covering as much or as little of the chain as you need:

- **Origin services** — collection from your supplier, export customs clearance,
  container stuffing and terminal handling
- **Ocean leg** — carrier booking, bill of lading issuance, and cargo insurance if you
  want it arranged
- **Destination services** — import customs clearance, port charges, and delivery to the
  consignee's door anywhere in the GCC

## Cargo we handle

General and palletised cargo, project and out-of-gauge shipments, temperature-controlled
reefer cargo, liquid bulk in flexitanks or ISO tanks, and food consignments requiring
municipality inspection. Hazardous cargo is accepted subject to class, UN number and
carrier approval — tell us at the quoting stage so we book the right equipment.

## Choosing between FCL and LCL

As a rough guide, once your consignment passes roughly 13–15 cubic metres, a 20ft
container usually becomes cheaper than paying LCL rates per cubic metre — and it removes
the consolidation and deconsolidation steps, which shortens transit. Below that, LCL is
almost always the better answer. We will quote both if it is close.
