Food is our deepest expertise. We operate packing facilities with food-safe handling and
manage the customs regime food consignments attract — a regime materially stricter than
for general cargo, where most costly mistakes are made before the goods ever ship.

## What the service includes

- **Temperature-controlled transport** — chilled, frozen and ambient, by reefer container
  and reefer truck
- **Packing and repacking** at our own facility, under food-safe conditions
- **Product and label registration** with the relevant authority
- **Health certificate and halal documentation** coordination
- **Customs clearance and inspection attendance**
- **Liquid bulk** in flexitanks and ISO tanks

## The three things that stop food consignments

1. **Label non-compliance** — missing Arabic text, missing importer details, or a
   production and expiry date format the inspector will not accept.
2. **Insufficient remaining shelf life** — shelf life is counted from production, not
   dispatch, and a long transit can push a consignment under the threshold on arrival.
3. **Missing or mismatched health certificates** — the certificate must match the
   consignment exactly: same product, same batch, same quantity.

All three are avoidable, and all three are cheaper to fix before dispatch. If you are
importing a food product for the first time, send us the label artwork and the
specification before you place the order — registration belongs at the purchase-order
stage, not when the cargo is on the water.

## Cold chain

Reefers are pre-cooled before stuffing and run at a single confirmed setpoint; we can
supply temperature logs on request. A reefer maintains temperature — it does not pull
down a warm load — so cargo must be at temperature before it is stuffed. A consignment
mixing frozen and chilled lines needs parallel units, not a compromise setting.

## What we need to quote

Origin and destination, the product, the temperature regime, the volume and weight, and
your ready date. For a first-time import, add the label artwork and ingredient list so we
can check registration before quoting.
