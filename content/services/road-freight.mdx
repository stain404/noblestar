Road is the workhorse of intra-GCC trade — faster than sea on short lanes, far cheaper
than air, and door to door without a terminal in the middle.

## What the service includes

- **Full-truck load (FTL)** — dedicated vehicle, direct routing, fastest transit
- **Shared load (LTL)** — scheduled departures on the main lanes, priced by space used
- **First and last mile** — collection and delivery within the UAE using our own drivers
- **Border management** — customs declarations, conformity certificates and transit
  paperwork prepared before the truck leaves

## Equipment

Curtain-side and box trailers for general cargo, flatbeds and lowbeds for machinery and
out-of-gauge loads, and reefers for food and pharmaceutical consignments. Tell us the
commodity, weight and dimensions and we will specify the right vehicle.

## Why border preparation matters

Most cross-border delays are documentation problems, not traffic. Saudi Arabia's SABER
conformity requirement in particular catches out shippers who have not registered their
products before dispatch — and it cannot be completed in a day. We check conformity,
origin and permit requirements at the booking stage, so problems surface in our office
and not at the border with a loaded truck waiting.

## What we need to quote

Origin and destination, the commodity, weight and dimensions, whether it is a full or
part load, and your ready date. For any lane into Saudi Arabia, tell us the product so we
can check where its conformity registration stands before quoting.
