Air freight is our top priority service. When a consignment goes by air, something
downstream is usually waiting on it — a production line, a tender deadline, a vessel
sailing — and we treat it that way.

## What the service includes

- **Space booking** across scheduled carriers and freighters
- **Export and import customs clearance** filed in-house
- **Pickup and delivery** through our own driver fleet and vetted partners
- **Charter and on-board courier** arrangements for genuinely critical cargo
- **Temperature-controlled and dangerous-goods** uplift, arranged to IATA rules

## When air is the right call

Air suits high-value, low-volume, perishable or urgent cargo. It is rarely right for
heavy, low-value goods, where the freight cost can exceed the value of the shipment. If
transit time is not critical, ask us for sea or road as well — we would rather quote
honestly than book the more expensive mode.

## Chargeable weight

Air freight is priced on **chargeable weight**: whichever is greater of the actual gross
weight or the volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm ÷ 6000). A light, bulky
pallet at 120 × 100 × 150 cm is charged as 300 kg however little it weighs on the scale.
This is the usual reason an air quote comes back higher than expected, and it is why we
ask for dimensions rather than weight alone.

## Getting a quote

Send the origin and destination, the gross weight, the dimensions of every piece, the
commodity, and your deadline. If the cargo is temperature-controlled or hazardous, say
so at this stage — send the safety data sheet and UN number with the enquiry.
