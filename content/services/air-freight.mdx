  - q: Is air freight priced by weight or by volume?
    a: By chargeable weight, which is the greater of the actual gross weight and the volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm, divided by 6000). Light, bulky cargo is therefore charged on volume. Send us dimensions as well as weight and we will calculate it for you.

Air freight shipments are treated as our top priority. When a consignment is going by
air, something downstream is usually waiting on it — a production line, a tender
deadline, a vessel sailing. We make sure every kilo air-freighted reaches its
destination safely and on time.

## What the service includes

- **Space booking** across scheduled carriers out of Dubai International (DXB), Al
  Maktoum (DWC), Abu Dhabi (AUH) and Sharjah (SHJ)
- **Export and import customs clearance** filed in-house
- **Pickup and delivery** through our own driver fleet in the UAE and vetted partners
  elsewhere in the GCC
- **Charter and on-board courier** arrangements for genuinely critical cargo

## When air freight is the right call

Air is the right answer for high-value, low-volume, perishable or urgent cargo. It is
rarely the right answer for heavy, low-value goods, where the freight cost can exceed
the value of the shipment. If you are weighing air against sea on a lane where transit
time is not critical, ask us for both — we would rather quote you honestly than book the
more expensive mode.

## Special handling

We arrange temperature-controlled uplift for pharmaceuticals and perishables, including
active and passive cold-chain packaging, and we handle valuable cargo under airline
secure procedures. For food consignments, see our
[food cargo service](/services/food-cargo).
